RunOrFit Flex Belt

Specification

  • Main pocket: 20 × 10 cm, zip closure, three-dimensional cut so it holds its shape when loaded
  • Also has: a second pocket for cards and cash, a hook-and-loop flap pocket for keys, and a side port for a headphone cable
  • Belt: elastic strap with a side-release buckle, adjustable to approximately 70 cm total
  • Reflective strip: across the front of the pouch
  • Material: nylon, with moulded plastic buckle and zip pull
  • Weight: approximately 85 g
  • Colours: Black, Blue, Lime, Grey, Orange, Pink
  • Country of manufacture: China

What is in the box

1 × running belt. Nothing else — no charger, no cable, no adapter, because none is needed.

Electrical and battery information

This product contains no battery and no electrical or electronic component.

Safety warnings

  • The reflective strip is a visibility aid, not safety apparel. It works when a driver's headlights fall directly on it. It is not certified high-visibility clothing and does not meet AS/NZS 1906.4 or any equivalent standard. In the dark, use lights and bright clothing as well.
  • Do not over-fill the pocket. It is sized for a phone, keys, cards and a gel. Forcing the zip past its capacity is what splits a seam.
  • Check the buckle before every run. Replace the belt if the buckle is cracked or no longer clicks firmly shut.
  • Not waterproof. The fabric is woven nylon and the zip is not sealed. It will resist light rain and sweat; it will not keep a phone dry in heavy rain or if submerged.
  • Wear it on the waist or hips only. Never place the strap around a neck, and keep it away from babies and children.
  • Keep packaging and plastic wrapping away from children — risk of suffocation.

Care

Rinse in cold water and wipe with mild soap after salty or sweaty use. Do not machine wash, tumble dry, iron or dry clean. Air dry fully, away from direct heat, with the zip open. Heat and machine agitation are what destroy the elastic and lift the reflective strip.

RunOrFit ImpactEase Insoles

  • Sizing: AU/EU 35–40 and 41–45, with trim lines moulded underneath, marked up to size 45

What is in the box

1 × pair of insoles. Nothing else.

These are not a medical device

These are comfort and cushioning insoles. They are not orthotics, they are not custom made, they are not prescribed, and they are not a medical device. We do not claim that they diagnose, treat, cure or prevent plantar fasciitis, flat feet, overpronation or any other condition.

If you have persistent foot pain, diabetes, reduced sensation in your feet, circulatory problems or a diagnosed foot condition, speak to a podiatrist or your doctor before changing what is inside your shoes.

How to fit them

  1. Take the existing insole out of your shoe and lay it on top of the new one, heels aligned.
  2. Cut along the trim line closest to your size. Cut less than you think — you can always trim again.
  3. Fit the new insole into the shoe, heel first, and press it flat.

Trim in small steps. An insole cut too short slides forward inside the shoe, and a cut cannot be undone.

Safety warnings

  • Stop using them if they cause pain, rubbing, numbness or a blister, and speak to a podiatrist if the problem continues.
  • Give yourself a few days to adapt. Anything new under your foot changes how your shoe fits. Start with short wear and build up.
  • Use one insole per shoe. Never stack these on top of an existing insole — it tightens the shoe, and a tight shoe causes blisters and nail damage.
  • Check the fit of the shoe afterwards. If your toes now press against the end, the shoe is too small with the insole in it.
  • Scissors are used to fit this product. Cutting is an adult job. Keep insoles, packaging and plastic wrapping away from children.
  • Replace them when the foam stops springing back. EVA compresses with use; once it stays flat it is no longer cushioning anything.

Care

Remove and air after long or sweaty use. Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap; do not machine wash, do not tumble dry and do not dry on a radiator or in direct sun — heat permanently deforms EVA. Let them dry completely before putting them back in a shoe.
