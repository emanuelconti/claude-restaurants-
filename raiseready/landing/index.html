FAQ

Is this investment advice?

No. We review structure, clarity, and common red flags in what you submit. We never advise on whether to invest, raise, or which terms to accept.

Who writes the report?

A structured review process combining an experienced finance/FP&A background with AI-assisted analysis, reviewed before delivery.

What if I don't have numbers/a financial model yet?

Choose Deck Check — it covers the deck only. You can add the Numbers Check later.

Do you guarantee I'll raise money / win the deal / get accepted?

No one honestly can. We help you remove avoidable friction before your reader sees it.

Is this only for startup founders raising money?

No. We review any deck where a critical reader decides fast: investor pitches, sales/client pitches, accelerator or competition applications, grant proposals.
