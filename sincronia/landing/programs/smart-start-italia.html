Smart&Start Italia

Invitalia · Italy

Rolling Type: 0% loan + grant portion
Status today · checked yesterday
Open-window scheme with no fixed deadline — applications assessed as they arrive, while funds last.

What you get

Zero-interest financing covering up to 80% of eligible costs, with a higher share and a partial non-repayable grant for startups based in southern Italy.

Who it is for

Innovative startups already registered, or being formed, with projects between €100k and €1.5M.

The catch

You must be registered in the 'startup innovative' special register, which has its own requirements.

Official source: https://www.invitalia.it/cosa-facciamo/creiamo-nuove-aziende/smart-start-italia Verified: 2026-09-13
