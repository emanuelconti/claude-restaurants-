Questions

Do you guarantee I'll get the funding?

No — and be wary of anyone who does. The awarding body decides. We help you apply to the right programs, with a stronger application.

Where does the data come from?

A database we maintain by hand against each authority's official source. Every row carries its link and the date we last checked it.

What if nothing fits me?

We tell you honestly instead of forcing weak matches. The database is verified but not exhaustive, and we say so in the report.

Sincronia provides information on public funding programs and editorial feedback on applications. We do not guarantee any funding, we do not give tax or legal advice, and every figure should be re-checked against the official source before you act on it.
