FAQ

Good to know

What is a "crew"?

A crew is a small group of friends — usually 2 to 6 people — who book and redeem perks together. Businesses get real groups showing up, not solo clicks.

Is SocialPerks free for businesses?

Yes — there's no upfront cost and no monthly fee during the beta. Businesses only pay a small fee per redemption, so you never pay for demand that doesn't show up.

How does matching work?

We match live campaigns to nearby crews based on location, past visits and stated interests, so the businesses on the platform reach people likely to actually redeem.

Which cities are you live in?

We're piloting across multiple cities in Europe, with new ones added as businesses join. Request beta access and we'll tell you if we're live near you.
