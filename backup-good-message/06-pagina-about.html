What we are

A small online-only studio selling to Singapore. No physical shop, no showroom, no collection point, no phone line — a small studio cannot staff one properly, and we would rather answer you clearly in writing than leave you on hold. Email reaches a person, and every email gets a reply within 2 business days, Monday to Friday.

Good Message Studio is established in Italy. Business and registration details are provided on request by email.

How the product reaches you

Orders are prepared and dispatched by our supplier’s warehouse in China, direct to your address in Singapore. Most orders arrive in 5–8 business days. Preparation normally takes 1–4 business days and tracked transit normally takes 4–8 days after dispatch; during peak periods, customs checks or carrier disruption, allow up to 13 days. These are estimates, not guarantees. We publish them in our Shipping Policy because you deserve to know before you buy, not after.

Tracked shipping is free, with no minimum spend, and the shipping charge at checkout is S$0.

What we will not do

Our signature Enjoy The Little Things Hoodie has a plain front and a blue message across the back. Alongside it, our collection includes everyday apparel and accessories. Each product page shows the design and options available for that item.

We do not show a crossed-out “was” price for an amount nobody was ever charged, and we do not publish review counts we do not have.

Our signature hoodie is available in XS–4XL, with a shorter body in XS–XL and a longer body in 2XL–4XL. Other items have their own size ranges and fit. Check the details on the individual product page before choosing.

Where we stand on returns

We do not accept change-of-mind returns, refunds or exchanges, and we say so before you buy rather than burying it. Please check the individual product’s sizing details and ask us any questions before ordering. Please choose carefully.

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, defective or not as described, your rights under Singapore’s Lemon Law rules are protected and nothing in our policies limits them. We arrange a free repair or replacement first; where that is impossible or disproportionate, a price reduction or refund applies.

Ask before you buy

For clothing, compare the product’s measurements with a similar garment you already own, laid flat. Between two sizes? Write to hello@goodmessagestudio.com and we will tell you which one we would send. We would much rather answer that question than handle a complaint we could have prevented.
