# ZenBudget — €200 Launch Plan

Goal: sell enough €19 copies to cover the €200 spend and turn a profit.
Personal-finance templates are one of the highest-converting digital product
categories on Gumroad/Etsy specifically because of free organic reach on
TikTok/Pinterest/Instagram — lean on that before paid spend.

## Audience

Broad consumer audience, 20–40 y/o, interested in budgeting, saving,
"get your finances together" content. This audience actively searches for
and shares these templates — a rare case where organic reach alone can carry
most of the launch.

## Step 1 — Organic first (days 1–7, €0)

This category sells primarily through short-form video, not ads. Spend the
first week here:

1. **TikTok/Instagram Reels**: 3–5 short videos scrolling through the
   Dashboard, filling in a transaction live, showing the savings-goal
   progress bar fill up. Hook: "I built the only budget spreadsheet I've
   actually used for 6 months" / "POV: your subscriptions are secretly
   costing you €600/year". Caption with a link to the landing page /
   Gumroad, or "link in bio".
2. **Pinterest**: create 5–8 pins (vertical images of the Dashboard and
   Subscriptions tab) — Pinterest has the longest organic tail of any
   platform for this niche, pins keep converting for months.
3. **r/personalfinance, r/budget, r/povertyfinance** (value-first — share the
   template's structure or a tip, not a sales pitch; many of these subs
   remove direct promotion, check rules first).
4. List on **Etsy** and **Gumroad Discover** (both free, both have organic
   search traffic for "budget spreadsheet template").

## Step 2 — Paid boost (days 7–21, €200)

| Channel | Spend | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok Ads — Spark Ads boosting your best organic video | €100 | Cheapest CPM for this demo, and boosting a video that already has proof of engagement converts far better than a cold ad. |
| Meta Ads (Instagram Reels placement) | €70 | Interest targeting: "personal finance", "budgeting", "Mint", "YNAB" (competitor apps), "saving money", age 22–40. |
| Pinterest Ads promoting your top-performing pin | €30 | Very cheap CPC in this niche, long conversion tail. |

**Total: €200**

## Ad copy (use as-is or adapt)

**Hook:** "I stopped guessing where my money went."
**Body:** "ZenBudget is the one spreadsheet — budget, savings goals, debt
payoff and subscriptions, all on one dashboard. $19, yours forever."
**CTA:** "Get the template"

## Timeline

- Days 1–7: organic content push, €0 spent, list on Etsy + Gumroad
- Day 7: boost whichever piece of organic content has the best
  engagement/click-through — don't guess, follow the data
- Days 7–21: monitor daily; kill anything under 1% CTR after €15 spent
- Day 21: reassess, reinvest profit if ROAS > 2 (typical for this price point
  and category when the creative is working)

## Honest expectations

At $19 with low production cost, this product only needs a small number of
sales to be profitable — roughly 11 sales covers the €200 spend before
Gumroad's ~10% fee, and this category's organic reach (TikTok/Pinterest)
routinely outperforms paid spend when the content is good. Treat the €200 as
a boost on top of organic traction, not a replacement for it.
