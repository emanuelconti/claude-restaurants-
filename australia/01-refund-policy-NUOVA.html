THE SHORT VERSION

We do not accept returns, refunds or exchanges for a change of mind, an accidental purchase, personal preference or an incorrectly chosen size when the item is functional and as described. This does not limit your mandatory statutory rights, which are set out below and which nothing in this policy excludes or reduces.

If an item is damaged, incorrect, defective or not as described, you have rights, and which remedy applies depends on where you live. Both are set out below.

IF YOU ARE IN AUSTRALIA

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.

  • Major failure. If the problem is a major failure — the item is significantly different from its description, is unsafe, is unfit for its normal purpose, or has a fault a reasonable person would not have accepted had they known about it — you choose. You are entitled to a full refund of the amount you paid, or a replacement of the same type and value. We will not deduct anything for the use you have had of the item, and we will not insist on a repair instead. You may also keep the item and ask for compensation for the drop in its value.
  • Minor failure. For a problem that is not major, we will remedy it within a reasonable time. Where the failure is minor, we may choose between repair, replacement or refund.
  • You are also entitled to compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage caused by the failure.
  • You do not need the original packaging to make a claim, and there is no time limit beyond what is reasonable for goods of this kind.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any guarantee, right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

IF YOU ARE IN SINGAPORE

Our goods are covered by the protections under Singapore’s Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act and the Lemon Law framework. For an approved defective or non-conforming item, we will first arrange a free repair or replacement. If that is impossible, disproportionate, or cannot be completed within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience, a price reduction or refund applies.

CHANGE OF MIND

Please review the published measurements and choose carefully before purchasing. We do not offer a change-of-mind period, and personal-preference returns, refunds and exchanges are not accepted. Neither Australian nor Singapore law requires a seller to accept a change-of-mind return, and this exclusion does not affect any of the statutory rights described above.

HOW TO MAKE A CLAIM

  1. Email hello@goodmessagestudio.com with your order number, a clear description of the issue, and clear photographs or a short video where useful. No separate form is required.
  2. We review the request and reply within 2 business days with specific instructions. If you are in Australia and the failure is major, tell us in that email whether you want a refund or a replacement — the choice is yours.
  3. Do not post anything back before we provide written instructions and the correct return address. Our registered office is not a return address.

DAMAGED, INCORRECT OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS

  • Transit damage or incorrect item: contact us within 7 days of delivery with photographs of the item and packaging.
  • Functional or hidden defect: contact us as soon as the fault appears, with clear evidence of the issue.

These timeframes help us investigate quickly. They do not cut short any statutory right that applies for longer.

EXCHANGES

We do not exchange items because of a change of mind or an incorrectly chosen size. A replacement is available for an approved damaged, incorrect, defective or non-conforming item, and is yours to choose where Australian law gives you that choice.

REFUNDS

Where a refund applies, it is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to display the credit.

CANCELLING BEFORE DISPATCH

If the order has not been handed to the carrier, email us immediately with your order number. We will cancel it and issue a full refund. Once the order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

ORDERS THAT DO NOT ARRIVE

Please see our Shipping Policy. If an order has not arrived within 25 business days of dispatch, contact us. A parcel confirmed lost in transit will be resent or refunded in full; this is a fulfilment failure, not a change-of-mind return.

QUESTIONS
