WHERE WE SHIP

We ship to Singapore and Australia. Orders are prepared and dispatched directly by our supplier’s warehouse in China.

SHIPPING COST

Standard tracked shipping is free to both countries, with no minimum spend. The shipping charge at checkout is S$0 / A$0. The price shown for the product is the full amount we collect for the item and standard delivery.

PROCESSING AND DELIVERY TIME

Singapore: most orders arrive in 8–14 business days from purchase.

  • Processing: normally 2–5 business days from purchase.
  • Transit: normally 6–10 days after dispatch.
  • Outer limit: during peak periods, customs inspections, adverse weather or carrier disruption, allow up to 18 business days.

Australia: most orders arrive in 10–18 business days from purchase.

  • Processing: normally 2–5 business days from purchase.
  • Transit: normally 8–14 days after dispatch.
  • Outer limit: during peak periods, customs inspections, adverse weather or carrier disruption, allow up to 25 business days.

If a product page states a different preparation estimate, that product-specific estimate applies. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays begin processing on the next business day. These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.

TRACKING

A tracking number is emailed as soon as your parcel is handed to the carrier. It can take several days for the first scan to appear, and tracking may remain unchanged for a number of days while the parcel is in transit.

TAXES AND IMPORT CHARGES

Singapore. Good Message Studio is not registered for Singapore GST, so we do not collect Singapore GST at checkout. For a typical non-dutiable air or postal consignment whose total CIF value does not exceed S$400, Singapore import GST relief generally applies.

Australia. Good Message Studio is not registered for Australian GST, because our Australian sales are below the A$75,000 registration threshold for overseas suppliers. No GST is collected at checkout. For imported goods valued at A$1,000 or less, customs duty and import processing charges do not normally apply.

In either country, if a carrier requests an unexpected payment, contact us before paying so we can verify it.

ADDRESS CHANGES

Please check your delivery address carefully before checkout. If you notice an error, email hello@goodmessagestudio.com immediately with your order number. We will try to update it, but changes cannot be guaranteed once processing has started. Additional postage may be required if a parcel is returned because the address supplied was incorrect or incomplete.

DELAYED, LOST OR DAMAGED PARCELS

Contact us with your order number if tracking has not updated for more than 10 business days, the parcel arrives visibly damaged, or tracking says delivered but nothing has arrived. Clear photographs of the packaging and product, or a short video, help us investigate. Report transit damage or an incorrect item within 7 days of delivery. Report a functional defect as soon as it appears.

If an order has not arrived within 25 business days of dispatch, contact us. If the parcel is confirmed lost, we will resend it or issue a full refund.

CANCELLATIONS

You may cancel an order only before it has been dispatched. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. Once the parcel has been handed to the carrier, the order cannot be cancelled and our Return and Refund Policy applies.

QUESTIONS

Email hello@goodmessagestudio.com. We reply within 2 business days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
